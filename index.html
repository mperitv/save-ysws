You Ship You Save

Build your own open-source project and ship it, we donate to Make-A-Wish Foundation to make children's wishes come true!!

Here's how it works

1. Install Hackatime for tracking hours or Lapse for track hours via record

2. Build your project inspired by dreams and wishes! You can make a dreamy website, a game set in a dream, or a robot which wishes good luck when you open your IDE!

3. Once you have built your project, ship it!

4. Reviewers will review your project and approve it for donation.

5. We will donate to Make-A-Wish Foundation to make children's wishes come true!

You ship a project that you made while tracking the hours with hackatime. You can build everything; a website, a game, a robot... both hardware and software are welcome

We ship a donation to Make-A-Wish Foundation to support their mission. This will help children's wishes come true, and you will be one of the people who make it possible!!
