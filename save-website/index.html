You Ship You Save

Build your own open-source project and ship it, we donate to Make-A-Wish Foundation to make children's wishes come true!!

Here's how it works

1. Track your hours +

Install Hackatime for tracking hours or Lapse for track hours via record

2. Build your project +

Build your project inspired by dreams and wishes! You can make a dreamy website, a game set in a dream, or a robot which wishes good luck when you open your IDE!

3. Ship your project +

Once you have built your project, ship it!

4. Get reviewed +

Reviewers will review your project and approve it for donation.

5. Wishes come true! +

We will donate to Make-A-Wish Foundation to make children's wishes come true, and you will be one of the people who make it possible!!
